# **🌊 AQUATIC SMP**

### **Official Server Concept & Development Plan**

*The Grand Seas • Pirate Crews • Island Conquest • The Pirate King*

---

## **1\. SERVER OVERVIEW**

**Aquatic SMP** is a pirate-themed Minecraft survival multiplayer server set in a vast ocean world filled with scattered islands, hidden treasures, pirate crews, and territorial conflicts.

Unlike traditional survival servers, Aquatic SMP revolves around piracy, exploration, conquest, alliances, and betrayal.

Every player is a pirate. Players form crews, construct ships, establish territories, raid rival crews, discover islands, and compete for dominance over the seas.

The ultimate goal is to become the **Pirate King**, the most powerful and respected pirate in the world.

### **Core Features**

* Ocean-based world filled with islands.  
* Player-created pirate crews.  
* Island ownership and conquest.  
* Custom ships and naval gameplay.  
* Open-world PvP and raiding.  
* Treasure hunting and exploration.  
* Bounty and wanted systems.  
* Server-wide Pirate King tournament.

---

## **2\. THE WORLD**

The world of Aquatic SMP will consist primarily of ocean, with numerous islands scattered throughout the map.

Rather than having a traditional Minecraft landscape with large continents, the ocean will serve as the main environment for exploration, transportation, and conflict.

### **Island Classifications**

| Island Type | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Tropical Islands | Common islands suitable for settlements and bases. |
| Jungle Islands | Islands containing valuable natural resources. |
| Volcanic Islands | Dangerous locations with valuable materials. |
| Snowy Islands | Unique environments and rare resources. |
| Desert Islands | Islands containing hidden treasures and structures. |
| Ancient Islands | Abandoned ruins containing secrets and valuable loot. |
| Capital Island | The central location for major server events and the Pirate King. |

### **World Design Goals**

* A large ocean with scattered islands.  
* Different island sizes and biomes.  
* Hidden locations and treasure.  
* Naturally generated and custom-built structures.  
* A central spawn island.  
* A custom world map showing discovered islands and territories.

---

## **3\. PIRATE CREWS**

Players will be encouraged to form pirate crews.

Crews will serve as the main social and political groups within Aquatic SMP.

Each crew will have its own identity, leadership, flag, ship, territory, and reputation.

### **Crew Hierarchy**

| Position | Responsibility |
| ----- | ----- |
| Captain | Leader of the crew. |
| First Mate | Second-in-command. |
| Navigator | Responsible for navigation and exploration. |
| Quartermaster | Manages resources and crew supplies. |
| Crew Members | General members of the crew. |

### **Crew Features**

* Create and manage crews.  
* Establish crew alliances.  
* Declare rivalries and wars.  
* Claim islands and establish bases.  
* Raid opposing crews.  
* Steal resources and treasure.  
* Create custom flags and symbols.  
* Build and maintain pirate ships.

Crews should initially have a recommended maximum of 5–6 members to encourage competition and prevent one crew from dominating the entire server.

---

## **4\. ISLAND OWNERSHIP & CONQUEST**

Island conquest will be one of the central mechanics of Aquatic SMP.

Players and crews will be able to establish territories, defend their islands, and attempt to conquer territory belonging to other crews.

The server will encourage freedom and player-driven conflict rather than relying entirely on scripted wars.

### **Territory Classifications**

**Unclaimed Islands**

* Available for crews to discover and occupy.  
* Can be claimed by any crew.  
* Can become the foundation of a new pirate territory.

**Occupied Islands**

* Controlled by an established crew.  
* Can display the crew’s flag and identity.  
* Can be attacked and potentially conquered.

**Neutral Islands**

* Designated locations for community events and meetings.  
* Can include trading areas and public facilities.  
* Provide locations where crews can interact without fighting.

### **Conquest Rules**

* Crews can raid opposing territories.  
* Players can engage in open-world PvP.  
* Crews can steal resources and treasure.  
* Alliances can be formed or broken.  
* Territory can change ownership through conquest.

Basic protections against offline destruction, spawn camping, and harassment will be necessary to keep competition fair.

---

## **5\. THE PIRATE KING**

The Pirate King is the highest title attainable in Aquatic SMP.

There can only be one official Pirate King at a time.

Unlike ordinary territory ownership, the title will be determined through a server-wide tournament.

### **The Pirate King Tournament**

**Stage 1: Registration**

* Crews register for the tournament.  
* Each crew selects its competitors.

**Stage 2: Qualifiers**

* Crews compete in preliminary challenges.  
* Successful crews advance.

**Stage 3: Elimination Rounds**

* Quarterfinals and semifinals.  
* Competing crews face increasingly difficult challenges.

**Stage 4: Grand Finale**

* The remaining competitors fight for the title.  
* The winning captain earns the crown.

**Stage 5: Coronation**

* The winner officially becomes Pirate King.  
* A server-wide ceremony celebrates the new ruler.

### **Pirate King Rewards**

* A unique Pirate King crown.  
* Access to a royal throne and capital.  
* Special server privileges.  
* Recognition across the server.  
* Permanent recognition in the Hall of Kings.

The Pirate King will retain the title until the next official tournament.

---

## **6\. CUSTOM PLUGINS & GAMEPLAY MECHANICS**

Aquatic SMP will use plugins to introduce mechanics that are not normally available in vanilla Minecraft.

The server will ideally run on Paper (Java Edition), depending on the final edition and hosting decisions.

### **Essential Plugin Systems**

| System | Purpose |
| ----- | ----- |
| Ship Mechanics | Allow players to construct and operate ships. |
| Crew Management | Create crews, assign ranks, and manage members. |
| Land Claims | Manage island ownership and territory. |
| Economy | Introduce a server currency. |
| Bounty System | Place bounties on notorious pirates. |
| Custom Items | Introduce unique pirate-themed equipment and treasures. |
| World Maps | Display islands and discovered territories. |
| PvP Management | Establish combat and raid rules. |

### **Signature Mechanics**

**Custom Ships**

* Construct ships using Minecraft blocks.  
* Allow ships to move across the ocean.  
* Introduce different ship sizes and upgrades.  
* Potentially include naval combat.

**Treasure Hunting**

* Hidden treasure chests across the world.  
* Rare loot and unique rewards.  
* Treasure maps leading to secret locations.

**Bounty System**

* Players can place bounties on other pirates.  
* Notorious players become wanted.  
* Successful bounty claims reward Doubloons.

**Crew Territories**

* Crews establish their own islands.  
* Territories display crew flags.  
* Ownership can change through conquest.

---

## **7\. SERVER EVENTS**

Regular events will keep Aquatic SMP active and give players objectives beyond ordinary survival.

| Event | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Treasure Hunt | A hidden treasure is placed somewhere in the world. |
| Pirate War | An official conflict between rival crews. |
| Bounty Hunt | Players compete to claim active bounties. |
| Lost Island | A newly discovered island containing rare loot. |
| King’s Challenge | A competition related to the Pirate King title. |
| Grand Raid | A major server-wide raid event. |
| Naval Battle | Crews compete using their ships. |

Events should be organized regularly by server staff.

---

## **8\. DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES**

The server should be developed in stages rather than attempting to implement every mechanic immediately.

### **Phase 1 — Foundation**

* Choose Minecraft edition.  
* Select server hosting.  
* Set up the server software.  
* Generate or acquire the ocean world.  
* Establish spawn and the central island.

### **Phase 2 — Core Gameplay**

* Install crew management plugins.  
* Implement island claiming.  
* Establish PvP and raiding rules.  
* Configure the economy.  
* Add basic ship mechanics.

### **Phase 3 — Custom Features**

* Introduce treasure hunting.  
* Add bounties.  
* Implement custom items.  
* Create the world map.  
* Develop naval combat.

### **Phase 4 — Launch Preparation**

* Test all plugins.  
* Establish server rules.  
* Recruit initial players.  
* Form the first pirate crews.  
* Prepare the opening event.

### **Phase 5 — Official Launch**

* Open the server.  
* Begin the first season.  
* Allow crews to establish territories.  
* Organize the first major server events.  
* Prepare for the first Pirate King tournament.
